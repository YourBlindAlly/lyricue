LyriCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

LyriCue is an audio lyric-prompting app built specifically for performers who cannot read printed lyrics, with accessibility for screen-reader users as a core part of its design.

What LyriCue stores on your device

Your songs, setlists, and preferences (voice selection, line-length settings, pedal key bindings, and similar) are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us and we do not have access to it.

What LyriCue sends to Dropbox, if you connect it

Connecting Dropbox is optional. If you connect it, LyriCue can read and write files in a dedicated LyriCue folder within your own Dropbox account, for three purposes: (1) letting you import songs you've stored there, (2) keeping a backup copy of your setlists in sync, and (3) automatically backing up a copy of any song you bring in through the Search feature, since that's the one way of adding a song that otherwise has no other copy anywhere. LyriCue cannot see or access any other part of your Dropbox account. Your Dropbox login credentials are handled entirely by Dropbox's own sign-in process. LyriCue never sees your Dropbox password.

What LyriCue sends to the Search feature, if you use it

Search is optional. If you use it, the text you type in is sent to our search backend, which looks the song up in a shared library of already-transcribed songs and returns the result — nothing else is sent, and the result is added only to your own personal library. The backend keeps a short-lived technical log (the search text and whether it succeeded) for a few days, used only to monitor and improve the feature. Song content itself is never logged or retained by us.

What LyriCue does not do

LyriCue does not collect analytics, does not track your usage, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your data with any third party. We do not have a server that stores your songs or setlists — everything lives on your device and, optionally, in your own Dropbox. The Search backend is stateless: it looks up a result and returns it, without keeping a copy for itself.

Children's privacy

LyriCue is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new "Last updated" date.
